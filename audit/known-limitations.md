# Known Hardware & OS Limitations (`audit/known-limitations.md`)

1. **Cross-App FPS Measurement Without Shizuku**: Android's security sandbox prevents normal third-party apps from reading SurfaceFlinger frame timestamps of other apps. Without `SHIZUKU_SHELL` (`dumpsys gfxinfo <pkg> framestats`), live frame-time percentiles measure the foreground Nexus Boost Choreographer pipeline (`CONFIDENCE: MEDIUM - LOCAL WINDOW`), whereas with Shizuku Shell active, target package `gfxinfo` framestats are queried (`CONFIDENCE: HIGH - GFXINFO`).
2. **Kernel Sysfs Write Restrictions on Stock Non-Root Kernels**: On locked-bootloader stock devices running Shizuku in Wireless Debugging mode (`UID 2000`), `/sys/devices/system/cpu/cpufreq/policy*/scaling_min_freq` is owned by `root:root` (`0644`). Nexus Boost detects this during capability scanning, marks direct sysfs clamping as `REQUIRES_ROOT`, and uses `cmd game` + `cmd power set-fixed-performance-mode-enabled` + ADPF instead.
3. **SELinux Policies on `/sys/class/thermal`**: Some OEM SELinux policies restrict reading certain vendor thermal zones from untrusted_app domain. Nexus Boost dynamically reads accessible zones and supplements them with `PowerManager.getCurrentThermalStatus()`, `getThermalHeadroom()`, and `BatteryManager` temperature.
